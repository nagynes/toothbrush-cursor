How does Liquid Pearl actually work?

Each Glint head is infused with Liquid Pearl, which is released gradually as you brush. Glint’s sonic vibrations help spread it across your teeth to lift everyday surface stains.

How is Glint different from a regular electric toothbrush?

Glint cleans like a great sonic toothbrush: it removes plaque and keeps your mouth fresh. The difference is the Liquid Pearl head and a vibration pattern designed around whitening.

Do I still need toothpaste?

Yes. Keep using your regular fluoride toothpaste. Liquid Pearl works alongside it, not instead of it.

Is Liquid Pearl safe for everyday use?

Liquid Pearl is designed for brushing twice a day, every day. If you have questions about your own teeth or gums, check with your dentist first.

Will my teeth really be whiter in 14 days?

Many people notice brighter teeth in as little as 14 days of brushing twice a day. Results vary with the kind of stains you have and habits like coffee, tea or smoking.

How long does each Liquid Pearl head last?

Each head holds enough Liquid Pearl for 30 days of brushing twice a day. After that, click on a fresh one.

Can I use Glint if I have sensitive teeth?

Glint’s bristles are soft and end-rounded. If your teeth are sensitive, talk to your dentist before starting any whitening routine.

Does Glint work on crowns, veneers, or fillings?

Glint cleans them like any good toothbrush. Whitening generally doesn’t change the colour of crowns, veneers or fillings, so ask your dentist if you’re unsure.

How long does the battery last?

Up to 100 days on one full charge, brushing two minutes twice a day.
